Framing

Balencia is an AI Life Coach — not a health app, not a fitness tracker. It connects every part of a user's life system (career, relationships, spirituality, finance, fitness, creativity, learning) and reveals the connections they can't see themselves.

Context: Direction Reset (2026-03-12) · Old UI/UX meeting (2026-04-23) · Current codebase
Codebase: 10 life domains · 80+ pages · 220+ services · 22 cross-pillar intelligence rules · 40+ proactive message types
01
Core Philosophy & Identity
1.1 What is the single sentence that describes what Balencia IS?

"If you are striving to improve yourself then Balencia is for you. Identity: Life Coach, not Health Coach." — "Balencia sees what we can't see."

● Open — Needs Salman's final wording
Recommendation

"Balencia is your AI life coach that connects your fitness, finances, faith, career, and relationships into one system — and shows you the patterns you'd never spot alone."

Codebase Rationale

The cross-pillar intelligence engine has 22 rules detecting connections across sleep, nutrition, exercise, stress, mood, and recovery. The knowledge graph tracks entity relationships across goals, habits, activities, and conditions. The system literally correlates WHOOP recovery data with spending patterns, mood logs with nutrition adherence, and workout consistency with career productivity.

1.2 "Balencia sees what we can't see" — what does this mean concretely in the UI?
● Open
Recommendation

All four approaches, layered by context:

  • Primary (daily): SIA proactive messages surface connections in plain language. 40+ proactive message types scored and top 2-3 delivered per day.
  • Secondary (home screen): "Insight of the Day" card from cross-pillar intelligence (e.g., HIGH_EXERCISE_LOW_SLEEP, NUTRITION_MOOD_CORRELATION).
  • Tertiary (weekly): Weekly digest summarizing "3 connections spotted this week" with before/after data.
  • Power user (on-demand): Knowledge Graph with node types (users, goals, habits, activities, exercises, foods, metrics, conditions) and edge types (causes, prevents, supports, conflicts_with).
The "Holy Shit" Moment

The first time SIA tells a user something true about their own life that they didn't consciously know — like "Your recovery scores drop 30% the week after you overspend." That's the product in one sentence.

02
The AI Coach (SIA) Experience
2.1 What is SIA's personality in 3 adjectives?

Brand guidelines: grounded, curious, warm, playful, quietly confident. Tone adapts by motivation tier.

Recommendation: Warm, Perceptive, Direct
  • Warm — Genuinely cares. Remembers your sister's wedding, your Ramadan goals, your shoulder injury.
  • Perceptive — The differentiator. Notices patterns: "You always skip evening routine when stressed at work."
  • Direct — Doesn't hedge. Clear recommendations adapted by motivation tier: gentle → structured → challenging.

What makes SIA distinct: Persistent memory (confidence-scored, evidence-based), personal wiki, 22 cross-domain rules, proactive messaging. She doesn't just answer — she initiates, remembers, and connects.

2.2 What does the SIA chat screen look like?
Recommendation: C) Chat with rich inline cards

The current chat already supports artifact generation, wiki page linking, check-in cards, memory/context cards, agent timeline, and transparency panel. SIA should show workout cards (tappable), meal plans, progress charts, and goal rings — all inline.

Mobile: Full-width cards. Desktop: Collapsible right panel for persistent context alongside conversation.

2.3 How does SIA connect life domains in conversation?
Recommendation
  • Proactively, always. Cross-domain observations should be the highest-priority message category.
  • Visual domain tags: Colored pills in messages — "Your ● Sleep quality drops 25% after days when your ● Finance stress is high."
  • Frequency: 1-2 cross-domain observations per session. Self-regulates via evidence-based rules.
2.4 How does SIA deep-link into features?
Recommendation: D) All of the above, context-dependent
  • Information/review → Rich card. Tappable card opens relevant screen.
  • Urgent action → Navigate. Direct navigation to the needed screen.
  • Future action → Schedule task. Creates a scheduled action for later.
2.5 Voice interaction — how prominent?
Recommendation: A) Prominent mic button + full-screen voice mode

Voice infrastructure is substantial: WebRTC calls, voice assistant page, call coach, Google Cloud TTS, call history, transcription support. Voice is lowest-friction input for the primary persona. The mic button should be prominent in chat, and full-screen voice mode should feel like calling a friend.

2.6 Should SIA have a visual avatar/face?
Recommendation: B) Simple animated icon/logo that pulses

3D avatars are expensive and age quickly. A beautifully animated abstract icon (the continuous stroke line, animated) gives SIA presence without uncanny valley risks. Pulses when listening, animates when thinking, settles when presenting.

2.7 What's the difference between "AI Coach" and "Chat"?
Recommendation: Merge into one unified SIA experience

Both /ai-coach and /chat share backend infrastructure (LangGraph, context injection, memory engine). The distinction confuses users. One SIA. One conversation. Multiple modes: text chat (default), voice mode, check-in mode, deep dive mode.

2.8 Proactive messages from SIA — where do they appear?
Recommendation: C) Both — push notification + home card

Push notification (short, intriguing) → tap → opens home screen card (insight) or SIA chat (conversation starter). Daily cap: 4 messages/day, timezone-aware (6am-10pm). No separate "insights feed" — that fragments the experience.

03
Onboarding & First Experience
3.1 How many screens should onboarding have?

Old meeting: ~3 screens. Direction Reset: 5-step flow. Current app: 7-step flow.

Recommendation: 5 screens (down from 7)
Welcome + Name (10 sec) — SIA greets, captures name
Life Focus (30 sec) — "What area needs attention?" → tap domain cards
Quick Assessment (60-90 sec) — 3-5 conversational questions about chosen domains
Plan Preview (30 sec) — AI generates plan, user reviews/tweaks
You're In (immediate) — Land on Today screen, optional integrations as enhancement cards

Deferred: Integrations, detailed preferences, deep assessment, body images — become "unlock deeper insights" prompts over first week.

3.2 Chatbot-driven or traditional screen-based onboarding?
Recommendation: C) Hybrid

SIA speaks at the top of each screen with personality, but actual input is structured — beautiful tappable cards, sliders, or simple selections. Best of both: personality + speed.

3.3 What information do you absolutely need before the user can start?
Recommendation

Mandatory: Name, life areas of interest (1-3 domains), primary life goal.

Deferred: Motivation level (SIA infers in 3-5 days), age/gender (asked when needed), integrations (prompted contextually), subscription.

Not needed: What they've tried before, biggest obstacle (SIA learns organically).

3.4 Should there be an intro video/animation from SIA?
Recommendation: C) Cinematic motion sequence — 5-10 seconds max

Abstract life domain icons connecting with the continuous stroke line, resolving into SIA's greeting. Think Apple product reveal, not explainer video. Skippable. The "wow" comes from the first cross-domain insight, not a pre-rendered video.

3.5 The AI assessment conversation — how deep?
Recommendation: B) 2-3 follow-up questions per goal

3 questions per domain × 1-2 domains = 3-6 questions total = ~90 seconds. Personal enough for a meaningful plan, fast enough to retain users. Deep assessment offered after 1 week of activity.

3.6 How do you handle the "I just want to try it" user?
Recommendation: D) SIA says "No worries, let's just start"

SIA IS the experience. Skip structured onboarding → land in conversation: "Tell me one thing on your mind today." Conversation insight extractor captures initial context. Minimum gate: Name + email only.

4.2 How do 8 life domains organize in the UI?
Recommendation: D) Hybrid — goals are primary, domains in Explore

Users think in goals, not domains. Each goal card shows domain tags. Domain dashboards available from Explore for those who want depth. SIA personalizes what appears on Today screen.

4.3 Pin-to-sidebar system — still want it?
Recommendation: Yes, desktop only. Max 5 pins.

Default pins based on onboarding choices (Fitness→Workouts, Nutrition→Meal Plan, Wellbeing→Journal, etc.). Max 5 to prevent sidebar creep.

4.4 The Explore page — what does it look like?
Recommendation: C) AI-curated recommendations first, full catalog below

"Recommended for you" (3-4 cards) at top based on goals + gaps. Full catalog below organized by domain with colored section headers. Search bar at top.

4.5–4.6 Mobile vs Desktop Navigation & Admin Panel
Recommendation

Mobile: Fixed 4-item bottom nav. Does NOT adapt — consistency builds muscle memory.

Desktop: Collapsible left sidebar with 5 primary + pinned modules. Both: SIA as universal navigation fallback.

Admin: Completely separate layout at /admin, role-gated. Different sidebar, different information density. Small "Admin" link in profile dropdown.

05
Home Screen / Daily Experience
5.1 What IS the home screen?
Recommendation: B) "Today" view — SIA's greeting + AI-suggested actions

Not a full chat (too aggressive), not a dense dashboard (too overwhelming), not customizable (too complex). A personal briefing: SIA's greeting at top, 2-3 suggested actions, one cross-domain insight, quick-access cards.

Think: Morning briefing from a personal assistant, not a dashboard full of charts.
5.2 Top 5 things visible on the home/today screen
Recommendation (in visual priority order)
SIA's greeting / proactive message — "Good morning, Amira. Recovery is strong — great day for that postponed workout."
AI-suggested actions — 2-4 domain-colored tappable cards across all life areas.
Active life goals with progress — Compact horizontal scroll of goal cards with progress rings.
Quick check-in prompt — "How are you feeling?" with quick-tap mood options.
Cross-domain insight — "This week: mood 40% higher on days you prayed all 5 prayers."
5.3 Data density & daily check-in
Recommendation: D) Adaptive by motivation tier
  • Low tier: Minimal — SIA's greeting, 1-2 actions, one insight. No charts.
  • Medium tier: Moderate — 3-4 actions, goal progress cards, quick check-in.
  • High tier: Dense — + health score, WHOOP metrics, schedule, streak stats.

Daily check-in: Part of SIA's greeting (conversational, not a form). Quick-tap mood options embedded in greeting card. One tap, done.

5.5 Cross-domain connections on the home screen
Recommendation: Layered — A + C + E
  • Insight of the day card — when cross-pillar engine has a high-confidence observation.
  • SIA mentions in greeting — "Sleep improved 20% since you resumed evening prayer."
  • Domain-colored tags on actions — small colored dots showing which domains each action touches.

Life Domains & Feature Organization
6.1 How do life domains present in the UI?
Recommendation: E) Combination of B + C + D
  • Colored tags everywhere — every action, insight, and goal shows domain colors (already defined in codebase).
  • Domain dashboards in Explore — drill-deep view for each domain.
  • Life wheel on Goals page — radar chart showing all domains with progress scores.
6.2 AI-first interaction — does it apply to ALL domains?
Recommendation: Yes, with varying AI-to-user ratios
  • AI-heavy (80%): Workouts, meal suggestions, daily schedule, learning recommendations.
  • Balanced (50%): Budget advice, journal prompts, habit suggestions, career actions.
  • User-heavy (20%): Spirituality, relationships, creative pursuits. SIA reminds and encourages but doesn't prescribe.

08
Knowledge System & Intelligence
8.1 Knowledge Graph — user-facing or behind the scenes?
Recommendation: C) Power-user feature in Explore

Technically impressive but visually complex. Available for power users; not promoted. The real value is what it enables — SIA's cross-domain insights. Users experience the graph through SIA, not the visualization.

8.2 Personal Wiki — visible to the user?
Recommendation: C) Partially — "What SIA knows about me"

Simplified view: key facts, preferences, patterns. E.g., "Prefers morning workouts," "Energy dips on Tuesdays." Each item has delete and "This is wrong" buttons (feeds to memory engine's processUserFeedback).

8.3–8.4 Cross-domain insight delivery & Memory transparency
Recommendations

Insights: E) All channels — SIA conversation, home card, weekly digest, insights page archive.

Memory: A) Full transparency with delete control. "What SIA remembers" page: browse all memories grouped by category, see confidence indicators, delete/correct/verify. Builds trust — transparency is a feature, not a risk.

Social & Community
9.1–9.4 Social prominence, Leaderboard, Competitions, & Accountability
Recommendations

Social: C) Minimal for v1. Basic accountability partner only. No feed, no public profiles.

Leaderboard: D) Remove for v1. Multi-domain ranking is nonsensical and potentially harmful.

Competitions: C) Deprioritize. Keep sophisticated backend intact; remove from primary nav.

Accountability: A) AI-driven for v1 (SIA already has commitment follow-ups, streak risk alerts, plan non-adherence). Social accountability in v2.

10
Gamification & Motivation
10.1–10.4 Streaks, Achievements, Micro-wins, & Tier-adaptive gamification
Recommendations

Streaks: B) Present but not central. One master streak on home; per-domain streaks in domain dashboards. "Balencia sees what you can't see" is the value prop, not "maintain your streak."

Achievements: Both cross-domain (unique to Balencia — "Life Connector," "Life Balanced," "Pattern Spotter") AND domain-specific ("30-Day Prayer Streak"). Cross-domain achievements celebrated more prominently.

Micro-wins: D) Adapted by tier. Low = celebrate immediately. Medium = SIA acknowledges naturally. High = batch at end of day.

Tier-adaptive gamification: Yes, confirmed. Low = heavy gamification (needs external motivation). Medium = moderate. High = light (self-motivated, focus on data). Transition between tiers is gradual.

11
Subscription & Monetization
11.1 Free vs paid access
● Open — Needs business decision
Recommendation

Free: 10 messages/day with SIA · Full Today screen · 2 life domains · Basic journal/mood/habits · 1 cross-domain insight/week

Paid: Unlimited SIA · Voice mode · All 10 domains · Full intelligence · Integrations · Analytics · Proactive messaging · Knowledge Graph

Key: Free tier delivers enough "holy shit" moments (1/week) to prove the concept and drive upgrades.
11.2–11.4 Pricing model, Locked features, & AI limits
Recommendations

Model: A) Pre-built tiers. Free / Pro ($9.99/mo) / Premium ($19.99/mo). Modular pricing undermines cross-domain value prop.

Locked features: E+D. SIA mentions naturally (consultative selling) + lock icon as fallback. Never hide features entirely — build desire.

AI limits: D) Tiered. Free = 10 messages/day (subtle counter). Paid = unlimited, no visible limits. Credit systems add cognitive load that undermines the SIA relationship.

Light mode: B) Available as toggle, dark is default. Some users need it; but dark IS the brand identity for all marketing and demos.

14
Notifications & Engagement
14.1–14.3 Top notifications, Push balance, & AI timing
Recommendations

Top 3 notifications: 1) SIA proactive AI insights (core product). 2) Streak alerts (time-sensitive). 3) Daily check-in prompt (drives habit).

Push balance: B+C. User configures per category; tier-based defaults. Low = 1/day. Medium = 2-3/day. High = 3-5/day. Never aggressive.

Proactive timing: Yes, already built. Runs every 3 hours, caps at 4/day, timezone-aware (6am-10pm), scores all types and picks top 2-3. Refine scoring to weight cross-domain insights highest.

15
Data, Privacy & Transparency
15.1–15.3 AI transparency, Data export, & Integration display
Recommendations

Transparency: "Show me the data" expandable on every SIA insight. Tap to see: data sources, time period, confidence level. Builds trust and differentiates from black-box AI.

Data export: Yes, essential. JSON + CSV export of all data. Required for GDPR, builds trust. Settings → Privacy → Export My Data.

Integrations: Both. Connected Services dashboard in Settings (status, sync time, connect/disconnect). Integration data silently woven into SIA's understanding and displayed in relevant contexts.

18
Open Questions & Wild Card
Q5 Community-created modules — scope for v1?
Recommendation: No.

Requires module framework/SDK, moderation tools, quality control, distribution, permissions. Massive distraction from core AI coaching. Revisit v2/v3 after product-market fit.

18.1 What experience should Balencia pioneer?
Recommendation

Cross-life-domain causal intelligence — already built, just needs to surface beautifully.

No app connects sleep → finance → relationships → career → spirituality and reveals causal patterns across all of them. The 22 cross-pillar rules, knowledge graph, memory engine, daily analysis, and conversation insight extractor are all built. The pioneering experience is making it visible in a way that feels magical, not clinical.

The pioneer moment: A user tracks 3+ domains for 2 weeks, then SIA says something so insightful about their life patterns that they think, "How did it know that?" — no other app delivers that.
18.2–18.3 What to keep & What must change
● Open — Needs Salman's input
Recommendations

Keep: The depth of AI intelligence backend — memory engine, cross-pillar intelligence, proactive messaging, comprehensive user context, knowledge graph. 220+ services of intelligence infrastructure. This is the moat.

Change: Navigation and feature density. 30+ sidebar items, 8 dashboard tabs, 10+ wellbeing sub-modules, two AI chat entry points. Reduce visible surface area by 70% while keeping 100% of capability — accessible through SIA and progressive disclosure.

18.5 What makes a user tell a friend about Balencia?
Recommendation

"It told me something about myself that I didn't know."

The moment SIA says: "I noticed that every time you skip your morning prayer, your productivity drops for the entire day — and three days later, you overspend online. These three things are connected."

That requires: 2+ weeks of multi-domain data, real cross-domain intelligence, SIA surfacing at the right time in the right tone, and a visual that makes the connection tangible.

Everything in this questionnaire should be designed to make this moment happen faster and more often.
